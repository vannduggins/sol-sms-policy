Sol SMS Test

Sol SMS Test is a privately operated personal-assistant notification service run by Vann Duggins under the registered SMS brand vann.

Service

Sol sends recurring, low-volume automated reminders, requested check-ins, account notifications, and service alerts only to its single account owner after that owner expressly opts in. It does not send marketing, bulk messages, or messages to third-party recipients, and it does not operate a public signup list.

SMS program

The sole account owner may enroll only through the public keyword instructions on the Text Opt-In page. No application-initiated SMS is sent before the owner texts START.
