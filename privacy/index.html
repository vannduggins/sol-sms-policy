Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

What this service is

Sol SMS Test operates under the registered SMS brand vann. It is a low-volume automated personal assistant notification service for one account owner. It is not a marketing program and does not accept public subscriptions.

Information used

The service uses the enrolled mobile number and the minimum message-delivery information needed to send and troubleshoot requested assistant check-ins. SMS content and delivery metadata are processed by Twilio and participating mobile carriers to deliver a message.

How information is used

Information is used only to send requested personal assistant notifications, handle delivery problems, and honor opt-out requests. Mobile information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Service providers such as Twilio and participating mobile carriers process only the information needed to deliver and support the messaging service.

Consent records

Consent is recorded when the enrolled account owner texts START from the mobile number being enrolled. The service retains the originating mobile number, keyword, and timestamp as evidence of consent and retains opt-out records as needed to prevent further messages.

Message frequency and choices

Message frequency varies and is expected to be low. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not transferable to another person or messaging program.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the service changes. The current version is posted on this page.
