Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

What this service is

Sol SMS Test is a low-volume personal assistant notification service for one account owner. It is not a marketing program and does not accept public subscriptions.

Information used

The service uses the enrolled mobile number and the minimum message-delivery information needed to send and troubleshoot requested assistant check-ins. SMS content and delivery metadata are processed by Twilio and participating mobile carriers to deliver a message.

How information is used

Information is used only to send requested personal assistant notifications, handle delivery problems, and honor opt-out requests. Mobile numbers and consent records are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency and choices

Message frequency varies and is expected to be low. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the service changes. The current version is posted on this page.
