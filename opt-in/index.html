Text Opt-In for Sol SMS Test

Sol SMS Test operates under the registered SMS brand vann. It is a recurring, low-volume automated personal-assistant notification program for one account owner. It does not accept public subscriptions.

How to opt in

During private one-to-one setup, the program operator gives the sole eligible account owner the dedicated Sol SMS number and directs the owner to this page. From the mobile number being enrolled, the owner voluntarily texts START to (336) 850-2295. No application-initiated message is sent until that START message is received. The originating mobile number, keyword, and timestamp are retained as the consent record.

Consent disclosure

By texting START, you agree to receive recurring automated Sol SMS Test personal-assistant reminders, requested check-ins, account notifications, and service alerts from vann. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Consent is limited to this program, is not transferable, and may be withdrawn at any time by replying STOP.

Confirmation message

"vann / Sol SMS Test: You are subscribed to recurring automated personal-assistant notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out."

Sol does not send marketing messages or maintain a public signup list.
