Sol SMS Test — SMS Opt-In

Sol SMS Test is a recurring, low-volume automated personal-assistant notification program operated by Vann Duggins under the registered SMS brand vann. Enrollment is limited to the program's single account owner.

Text-message opt-in advertisement

From the mobile number being enrolled, text START to (336) 850-2295 to subscribe.

By texting START, you agree to receive recurring automated Sol SMS Test personal-assistant reminders, requested check-ins, account notifications, and service alerts from vann. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

How consent is recorded

No application-initiated SMS is sent before the inbound START message is received. The originating mobile number, keyword, and timestamp are retained as the consent record.

This page is the program's only opt-in method. Consent is specific to Sol SMS Test, is not transferable, and may be withdrawn at any time by replying STOP. Sol does not accept verbal, paper, in-person, form-based, or third-party opt-ins.
